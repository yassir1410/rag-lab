Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an artificial intelligence framework for improving the quality of LLM-generated responses by grounding the model on external sources of knowledge.

Traditional large language models suffer from knowledge cutoffs and lack access to proprietary company data. They may also hallucinate plausible-sounding but factually incorrect assertions when prompted with questions outside their training distribution.

By decoupling knowledge retrieval from language generation, RAG systems ensure that answers are strictly based on verifiable source passages, complete with attribution citations and high domain fidelity.
